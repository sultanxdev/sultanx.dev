> **Active Development** — Dermo is being built as a managed AI employee for clinics, handling WhatsApp conversations, lead capture, appointments, and human handoff.

## Overview

Dermo is a managed AI employee for clinics that handles customer conversations through WhatsApp.

Instead of asking clinics to build and operate their own AI system, Dermo manages the AI, knowledge retrieval, integrations, deployment, monitoring, and maintenance.

The core workflow is:

**WhatsApp → AI → Lead → Appointment → Human Handoff**

Dermo is designed around a single-clinic deployment model rather than a generic multi-tenant SaaS platform.

## The Problem

Clinics receive repetitive enquiries about:

* consultation fees
* services
* doctors
* working hours
* appointments
* cancellations
* rescheduling
* treatment information

Handling these conversations manually creates delayed responses, repetitive receptionist work, missed leads, and inconsistent follow-up.

Dermo automates these interactions while keeping clinic staff involved whenever human judgment is required.

## What I'm Building

Dermo sits between the patient, the clinic, and its operational systems.

A typical conversation looks like:

```text
Patient
  ↓
WhatsApp
  ↓
Dermo AI
  ↓
Answer / Retrieve Information
  ↓
Capture Lead
  ↓
Check Availability
  ↓
Book Appointment
  ↓
Confirm
```

For conversations that require human involvement:

```text
AI
 ↓
Human Handoff
 ↓
Clinic Staff
```

The AI does not invent clinic pricing or appointment availability. Business information and transactional state are controlled by backend services.

## AI Architecture

Dermo uses a structured AI pipeline rather than relying on a single large prompt:

```text
Message
  ↓
Intent
  ↓
Safety
  ↓
Conversation State
  ↓
RAG / Business Data
  ↓
Tool Calling
  ↓
Response Validation
  ↓
WhatsApp
```

The LLM handles language understanding and reasoning.

Backend services control business state such as appointments, availability, services, leads, payments, and conversation mode.

## RAG

Clinic-approved information is stored and retrieved through PostgreSQL + pgvector.

```text
Clinic Knowledge
      ↓
Chunk
      ↓
Embed
      ↓
pgvector
      ↓
Retrieve
      ↓
LLM
      ↓
Grounded Response
```

This allows clinic information to change without hardcoding every answer directly into the prompt.

The goal is simple:

> **The AI should answer from approved clinic knowledge, not invent clinic facts.**

## Controlled Tools

The AI can request controlled operations such as:

```text
get_service_information()
get_available_slots()
create_lead()
create_appointment()
cancel_appointment()
reschedule_appointment()
create_handoff()
```

Backend services validate every operation before changing application state.

Critical operations such as booking, cancellation, payment handling, and human takeover remain deterministic.

## Appointment Consistency

Appointments are treated as transactional operations.

The system calculates availability from doctor schedules, working hours, breaks, and configured slot durations.

During booking, availability is checked again before the appointment is committed.

The goal is simple:

> **Never confirm an appointment that the system cannot actually create.**

## Human Handoff & Safety

Dermo can hand conversations to staff when:

* the patient explicitly requests a human
* medical input requires staff involvement
* the AI cannot answer reliably
* an emergency signal is detected

The conversation has an explicit operating mode:

```text
AI
HUMAN_TAKEOVER
```

When staff takes over, automated AI responses stop until the conversation is released back to the AI.

Dermo is not designed as a diagnostic or prescription system. Medical and emergency scenarios are handled through explicit safety rules and escalation paths.

## Payments

Dermo integrates appointment deposits into the booking workflow through Razorpay.

```text
Appointment Request
       ↓

       ↓
Deposit
       ↓
Payment Confirmation
       ↓
Appointment Confirmation
```

Payment events such as successful captures, failures, and refunds are handled through backend webhooks.

## Architecture

Dermo uses a modular monolith:

```text
Next.js Dashboard
        ↓
Express.js API
        ↓
Business Services
        ↓
PostgreSQL + pgvector
        ↓
AI / RAG / Tools
        ↓
WhatsApp / Razorpay
```

The architecture keeps business-critical logic inside the backend while allowing the AI layer to remain constrained and replaceable.

The project is structured as a monorepo so the dashboard, API, shared contracts, and supporting packages can evolve together.

## Security

Security is treated as part of the application architecture.

Sensitive credentials such as:

```text
WhatsApp Tokens
LLM API Keys
Razorpay Secrets
Database Credentials
```

remain server-side and are managed through environment configuration.


Sensitive operational events such as human takeover, appointment changes, refunds, and schedule changes are recorded through audit logging.


The AI layer is also evaluated against a dedicated scenario suite covering its supported intent categories and multilingual inputs.

The documented benchmark includes:

```text

English + Hindi + Hinglish
Medical Safety Guardrail Coverage
Intent Classification Coverage
```

## Why Dermo Exists

Most AI projects stop at:

**User → Prompt → LLM → Response**

Dermo explores a more realistic system:

**User → External Channel → Webhook → State → AI → Retrieval → Safety → Transaction → External API → Audit Trail**

The objective is to make AI useful inside a real operational workflow without forcing the clinic to understand or operate the underlying infrastructure.

> **Give clinics an AI employee they can use without having to operate the technology behind it.**
